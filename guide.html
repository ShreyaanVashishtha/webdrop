1. Quick Start: Beaming Files in 3 Steps

Zero accounts, zero configuration, zero cloud uploads.

1

Open WebDrop

Open WebDrop on both devices (phone, laptop, tablet). Each device generates an ephemeral 5-digit PIN and activates local radar discovery.

Works in Safari, Chrome, Edge & Firefox
2

Pair Devices

Tap the nearby device icon on the radar, type their 5-digit PIN in Manual Connect, or scan their QR code with your phone camera. Accept the connection prompt.

Direct encrypted WebRTC peer link
3

Drop & Beam

Drag-and-drop or browse files (photos, 4K videos, zip files, folders, or docs) or paste text snippets. Files transfer directly device-to-device at full Wi-Fi speed!

Fast flow-controlled P2P transfer

2. iPadOS & iOS Sharing Workflows

Cross-Platform AirDrop

Transfer photos, 4K videos, documents, and folders effortlessly from your iPad and iPhone to any device.

Why don't PWAs show in the native iPad/iPhone Share Sheet app row?

Unlike Android (which supports the W3C Web Share Target API and registers PWAs directly into the OS share menu), Apple has not implemented the Web Share Target API in iOS or iPadOS. Apple strictly restricts the native Share Sheet app row exclusively to native App Store binaries compiled with Xcode.

Even though Apple restricts PWAs from the Share Sheet app row, iPadOS and iOS support four faster, seamless native workflows built right into WebDrop:

4 Native iPad & iPhone Sharing Workflows

1

iPad Split View & Slide Over (Fastest!)

Native Drag & Drop

On your iPad, open WebDrop. Swipe up from the bottom edge to reveal the iPad Dock, then drag Photos or Files into Split View (side-by-side) or Slide Over.

Action: Select any batch of photos, videos, or folders in Photos/Files and drag them straight across into WebDrop's drop zone.
2

Native Photo Library & File Picker

Tap to Browse

Inside WebDrop, tap "Browse Files" or anywhere inside the dashed drop zone. iPadOS/iOS immediately opens Apple's system picker:

  • • Photo Library: Multi-select photos, 4K videos, and albums.
  • • Choose Files: Browse iCloud Drive, On My iPad, USB drives, & Downloads.
  • • Take Photo: Capture live photos/video directly into the queue.
3

Clipboard Copy & Paste

Command+V / Tap to Paste

In Apple Photos or Files, tap Share → Copy (or press ⌘C with an iPad keyboard). Switch to WebDrop and press ⌘V or tap "Paste Clipboard".

WebDrop automatically detects copied photos, files, and text snippets directly from system clipboard memory!
4

Apple Shortcuts Share Sheet Bridge

Custom Share Sheet Action

While Apple doesn't allow PWAs in the top app row, Apple Shortcuts DO appear in the Share Sheet action list below:

  1. Open the Apple Shortcuts app and create a new Shortcut.
  2. Enable "Show in Share Sheet" in Shortcut Details.
  3. Add the action "Copy to Clipboard", followed by "Open App: WebDrop".

3. Progressive Web App (PWA) Installation

Install WebDrop for a native standalone app experience on any OS.

Apple iOS / iPadOS

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

  1. Open WebDrop in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button () at the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  5. WebDrop will now run in full screen without browser bars and handle file sharing!

Android

Google Chrome, Edge, Samsung Internet

  1. Open WebDrop in Chrome.
  2. Tap the "Install app" banner at the bottom or tap the menu ().
  3. Select "Install WebDrop" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm the installation prompt.
  5. WebDrop integrates into your app drawer and native Android Share Sheet!

macOS (MacBook, iMac, Mac mini)

Safari (Sonoma / Sequoia) & Chrome

  1. Safari: In the top menu bar, click File → "Add to Dock..." → Click Add.
  2. Chrome / Brave: Click the Install icon in the address bar () or Menu → Save and share → Install WebDrop.
  3. Launches as a dedicated standalone Mac window in your Dock!

Windows 11 / 10

Microsoft Edge & Google Chrome

  1. Open WebDrop in Edge or Chrome.
  2. Click the App Available / Install button in the right corner of the address bar.
  3. Click Install.
  4. Optionally pin WebDrop to your Windows Taskbar or Start Menu.

4. Advanced Power Features

Under the hood capabilities engineered for ultimate performance.

Reliable Flow-Controlled P2P Transfer

WebDrop employs sequential 256 KB chunk slicing with Stop-and-Wait ACK flow control directly over WebRTC DataChannels. This prevents buffer overflows, guarantees exact byte ordering, and eliminates dropped packets.

100% resilient across all desktop and mobile browsers with zero cloud relays.

60 FPS Ultra-Low Latency Screen Cast

Stream your display, an active application window, or a specific browser tab live to all connected devices in the mesh room. Perfect for impromptu meetings, slideshows, code review, or collaborative debugging.

Hardware-accelerated VP8/VP9 video encoding with real-time peer playback.

Zero-Internet Airplane Mode (QR Handshake)

No Wi-Fi? No cellular signal? In an air-gapped secure environment or on a plane? WebDrop features visual optical SDP signaling. Devices exchange WebRTC handshake tokens purely via animated camera QR scanning.

100% offline, decentralized, and zero internet required.

Zero-Knowledge E2EE & Safety Fingerprints

In addition to DTLS/SCTP wire encryption, files are encrypted at the application layer with AES-256-GCM via ephemeral ECDH P-256 key exchange. Compare cryptographic safety emojis and numbers to ensure zero tampering.

Keys are generated locally in RAM and destroyed upon session disconnection.

5. Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions and helpful tips for seamless transfers.

Why don't I see WebDrop in the Share Sheet on my iPad or iPhone?

Apple's iOS and iPadOS operating systems do not support the W3C Web Share Target API for Progressive Web Apps (PWAs). Apple strictly restricts the native Share Sheet app row exclusively to native App Store binaries compiled with Xcode. On Android, installed PWAs automatically register as native share targets, but Apple has not yet enabled this for WebKit on iOS/iPadOS.

Best iPad workflow: Use iPadOS Split View or Slide Over! Swipe up from the bottom of your iPad screen to open Photos or Files side-by-side with WebDrop, and drag files directly into the WebDrop drop zone. You can also tap "Browse Files" in WebDrop to open the native Apple Photo Library and Files picker, or use Apple Shortcuts as a custom action.

My devices are not discovering each other on the radar. What can I do?

Radar discovery relies on devices sharing a common public IP address (such as being connected to the same home or office Wi-Fi network). If devices are on different networks (e.g. one on Wi-Fi and one on cellular), simply use the 5-digit PIN or click Copy Invite Link or scan the QR Code to connect immediately.

Is there any file size limit?

No. WebDrop supports large files (including 10GB+ videos, disk images, and massive archives). WebDrop streams file chunks directly using the browser's sandboxed Origin Private File System (OPFS), preventing high memory usage or tab crashes.

Do files touch any server during transfer?

Never. All data packets travel directly between devices through peer-to-peer WebRTC connections. WebDrop maintains zero file storage, zero logging, and zero user accounts.
